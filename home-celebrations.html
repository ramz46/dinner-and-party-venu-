Two Distinct Celebrations

Bespoke Atmospheres Tailored to Your Milestone

The Rehearsal Dinner

Intimate, Heartfelt & Unhurried

Typically hosted for immediate family, wedding parties, and cherished out-of-town guests the evening before the wedding. Our rehearsal experience prioritizes seated fine dining, personal speech acoustics, and deep connection.

  • Speech-Friendly Acoustics: Precision sound with wireless roving mics so every parent's memory is heard clearly.
  • 3 or 4-Course Plated Banquets: Seated family dining with wine pairings that allow relaxed conversations.
  • Rehearsal Run-of-Show Assistance: Our maître d' coordinates arrivals directly following church or garden rehearsal.

The Engagement Soirée

Effervescent, Social & Joyful

Announce and celebrate your proposal with friends and extended circles. Designed for lively cocktail hours, interactive tasting stations, champagne towers, and dancing under the stars.

  • Signature Cocktail Stations: Bespoke His & Hers cocktails crafted with artisanal syrups and dried botanicals.
  • Champagne Tower Pouring: A show-stopping photo centerpiece with sparkling vintage flutes.
  • Lounge & Veranda Mingling: Plush velvet lounge vignettes and outdoor courtyard fire bowls.

Pacing & Flow

Sample Celebration Timelines

See how seamlessly an evening unfolds at The Rosewood with our curated run-of-show.

1
5:30 PM – 6:15 PM

Courtyard Welcome & Sparkling Flutes

Guests arrive directly from ceremony rehearsal to the Veranda Courtyard. Passed chilled French Champagne and delicate gougères set a relaxed tone as bridal parties mingle.

2
6:15 PM – 6:30 PM

Transition & Welcome Toast by Hosts

Guests are invited under the iron-framed arches of The Glasshouse Conservatory. The host or parents of the groom deliver the opening welcome toast before dinner service commences.

3
6:30 PM – 8:15 PM

3-Course Plated Banquet with Sommelier Pairings

A culinary symphony of Hudson Valley organic fare served family-style or individually plated. Paced intentionally with breaks between courses for speeches by the best man and maid of honor.

4
8:15 PM – 9:30 PM

Dessert Salon, Digestifs & Gift Exchange

Warm espresso bar, miniature lemon tarts, and digestifs in the Cellar Lounge. An intimate window for the couple to present wedding party thank-you gifts in privacy.

1
6:00 PM – 7:00 PM

Cocktail Arrival & Artisanal Grazing

Bespoke signature cocktails poured over crystal hand-carved ice. An expansive 14-foot Hudson Valley artisanal cheese, charcuterie, and honeycomb table greets entering friends.

2
7:00 PM – 7:30 PM

The Grand Champagne Tower Toast

The couple pours the ceremonial top glass of the 5-tier champagne tower. Heartfelt engagement congratulations, cheers, and golden hour portrait sessions under the wisteria terrace.

3
7:30 PM – 9:00 PM

Interactive Chef Stations & Passed Small Plates

Handmade truffle gnocchi in parmesan wheels, fresh oysters on half-shell, and gourmet sliders circulated continuously by white-glove servers.

4
9:00 PM – 10:00 PM

Courtyard Music, Firebowls & Sweet Treats

Outdoor acoustic guitar or curated vinyl sounds around warm firebowls with signature s'mores tartlets and craft nightcaps.
