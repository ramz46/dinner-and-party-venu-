A Century of Botanical Romance & Heartfelt Gatherings

The Night Before Deserves Its Own Sanctuary

Weddings are grand celebrations for the world. But the rehearsal dinner is where the real intimacy lives—where toasts bring tears, laughter lingers across candlelit tables, and two families truly become one.

Our Origin

We Built the Sanctuary We Wished We Had for Our Own Rehearsal

In autumn 2017, founders Eleanor & Marcus Vance were planning their own wedding weekend. They dreamed of an unhurried rehearsal dinner: a candlelit room where Marcus’s 84-year-old grandmother could converse without competing against kitchen clatter, where their blended families could actually bond over reserve wine, and where toasts didn't come with an expiration time.

Instead, they encountered the same dilemma every couple faces: either cramped restaurant backrooms with noisy busboys and strict 2-hour dining caps, or cavernous hotel ballrooms that felt sterile and impersonal.

“We walked into the cool night air feeling rushed and thought: why is the most emotionally charged evening of the wedding treated like an appetizer? The night before should be a warm hearth, not a countdown clock.”

That realization became a four-year restoration mission. When they discovered the forgotten 1912 winter conservatory estate tucked into the Hudson Valley hillside, they knew they had found the sanctuary.

Our Guiding Truths

What Makes the Night Before Irreplaceable

We designed every square foot of The Rosewood around the real, vulnerable human dynamics of milestone gatherings.

Your Inner Circle Deserves Time

On your wedding day, 200 guests means an average of 45 fleeting seconds per person. The rehearsal dinner is your only window of four unhurried hours to sit across from your college roommates, hug cousins who flew across continents, and share memories that don't need a loudspeaker.

Toasts Need Room to Breathe

The funniest, most tear-inducing stories never happen during the 4-minute wedding reception limit. Here, toasts are conversational rituals. With our acoustic engineering hidden behind historic oak beams, whispers carry warmth and laughter doesn't turn into a wall of reverb.

Strict Single-Event Exclusivity

We never run a "second seating" or host two parties at once. The walled gardens, candlelit glasshouse conservatory, and subterranean cellar lounge belong solely to your family for the entire evening. When you arrive, the gates close to the world.

A Century in Bloom

The Journey of The Rosewood Estate

From an eccentric botanical conservatory to New York’s most cherished rehearsal dinner haven.

1912
The Founding

Horticultural Winter Garden Commissioned

Industrialist and botanist Arthur Van Der Bilt commissions European glassmakers to build a private glasshouse on his Hudson Valley hilltop. Hand-rolled Belgian glass transoms and iron fretwork are transported by river barge to house rare orchids, night-blooming jasmine, and winter citrus.

1948
The Subterranean Vault

The Limestone Wine Cellar is Excavated

Beneath the veranda terrace, an 800-bottle barrel cellar and tasting grotto are hand-carved from native bluestone and field brick, providing natural 56°F temperature control for seasonal cider pressings and French vintages.

2017
The Discovery

Eleanor & Marcus Find the Overgrown Gem

Following their own frustrating search for an intimate pre-wedding dinner venue, the Vance family discovers the estate. Though dormant for decades, the soaring glass arches, weeping wisteria, and brick courtyards remain structurally intact.

2018
The Restoration

A Four-Year Architectural Renaissance

Over 1,400 historic glass panels are lovingly restored. Radiant in-floor stone heating is installed for cozy winter events, whisper-quiet acoustic baffles are woven into the historic ceiling ironwork, and a state-of-the-art scratch culinary kitchen is built behind the conservatory.

2022
The First Evening

Our First Rehearsal Dinner Under the Stars

Forty-two guests gather for an October rehearsal dinner. Candlelight flickers against autumn glass, the cellar fire burns bright, and the couple stays late into the night sharing stories around the courtyard fire pits.

Today
Present Day

Voted Best Rehearsal Dinner Venue in New York State by The Knot and featured in Vogue Weddings, our promise remains unwavering: one celebration per evening, uncompromised culinary craft, and genuine human connection.

Our Promises

Hospitality Without Pretense or Compromise

Generational Accessibility

Every corner of the conservatory and veranda offers zero-threshold step-free access, wide paths, and quiet velvet seating alcoves so elderly family members feel revered and comfortable all evening.

Dietary Empathy

Vegan, celiac, kosher-style, or halal dietary needs are never treated as an inconvenience. Chef Julien prepares custom courses with equal artistry so no guest feels singled out or under-catered.

Stewardship of the Valley

We operate a 100% composted kitchen, zero single-use plastics, estate honey beehives, and bird-friendly restored glass to protect migrating Hudson River birds.
