Service Spotlight

Bespoke Culinary & Private Dining Experiences

An elevated culinary journey designed by Executive Chef Julien Beauchamp and Master Sommelier Antoine Laurent, tailored to the unique narrative of your celebration.

Farm-to-Table Philosophy

Rooted in Hudson Valley Terroir

We believe a rehearsal dinner menu should linger in your guests’ memories long after the wedding cake is cut. Every ingredient is sourced within a 60-mile radius of the estate: heirloom carrots from Kinderhook, grass-fed prime beef from Millbrook, and organic microgreens harvested right inside our own greenhouse beds.

Courses are synchronized gracefully with your evening's speeches. Rather than rushed conveyor-belt banquets, our kitchen paces each plate intentionally so toasts, laughter, and sips of wine unfold organically.

A Symphony of Seasonal Courses

Here is an example of our celebrated 4-course seated rehearsal dinner tasting.

First Course · Hors d'Oeuvre

Heirloom Burrata & Compressed Figs

Hudson Valley artisanal burrata, mission figs, aged Modena balsamico, toasted pine nuts, and estate-grown micro basil.

Wine Pairing: Domaine Vacheron Sancerre Blanc 2022
Second Course · Primi

Hand-Rolled Black Truffle Agnolotti

Ricotta di bufala, brown butter emulsion, shaved Umbrian black winter truffles, and crispy sage blossoms.

Wine Pairing: Vietti Roero Arneis 2021
Third Course · Main Entrée (Choice of)

Chilean Seabass or Braised Short Rib

Pan-seared Chilean Seabass in saffron fumet, OR 12-hour red wine braised Prime Angus short rib with parsnip silk.

Wine Pairing: Château Mont-Redon Châteauneuf-du-Pape 2019
Fourth Course · Dolce

Valrhona Dark Chocolate Ganache Tart

Smoked sea salt caramel, edible 24k gold leaf flakes, and house-churned Tahitian vanilla bean gelato.

Wine Pairing: Taylor Fladgate 20-Year Tawny Port
