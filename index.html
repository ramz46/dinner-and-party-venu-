The Rosewood Experience

Crafted for Life’s Most Intimate Prelude

Your rehearsal dinner and engagement party should never feel like an afterthought. At The Rosewood, we dedicate our entire grounds to intimate celebrations where families unite, heartfelt toasts echo without rush, and exceptional hospitality takes center stage.

Sole Event Exclusivity

We host only one celebration per evening. The entire glasshouse, walled gardens, and wine lounge are exclusively yours with zero distractions.

Chef-Driven Banquets

From family-style pasta feasts to 5-course seasonal pairings, our menus feature organic produce from Hudson Valley growers paired with rare reserve vintages.

Seamless Coordination

Our dedicated rehearsal concierge coordinates audio for speeches, manages custom menu placecards, arranges photo moments, and oversees timeline flow.

Because Friday and Saturday evenings coincide with prime Hudson Valley wedding calendars, we recommend reserving your rehearsal dinner or engagement soirée 6 to 12 months ahead. Sunday through Thursday dates often have flexible 3 to 6 month booking windows.
