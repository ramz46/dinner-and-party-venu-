Culinary & Event Packages

Thoughtfully Composed Celebrations

Choose from three signature celebration packages tailored to intimate plated dining, effervescent cocktail soirées, or grand weekend galas.

Intimate Seated Dining

The Twilight Rehearsal

Crafted for rehearsals of 25 to 85 guests

$ 145 / guest

A warm, unhurried 4-hour celebration focused on family-style seated dining, heartfelt toasts, and fine wine.

  • Welcome flute of French Champagne upon guest arrival
  • 3-Course seasonal Hudson Valley plated dinner
  • Sommelier-selected red & white reserve wine pairings
  • Candlelit tablescape with brass tapers & floral buds
  • Dedicated Maître d' for run-of-show & speech mic handling
Dietary Note: Gluten-free, vegan, kosher-style, and celiac plates accommodated seamlessly.
Most Celebrated
Cocktail & Action Stations

The Golden Hour Soirée

Signature for engagements of 35 to 120 guests

$ 165 / guest

An effervescent, high-energy party featuring hand-carved ice cocktails, passing canapés, and a 5-tier champagne tower.

  • 4-Hour Premium Open Bar with 2 custom couple cocktails
  • 6 Butler-passed savory hot & chilled canapés
  • 2 Interactive chef action stations (Pasta wheel + Raw bar)
  • 5-Tier Champagne Tower celebratory pouring experience
  • Lounge vignette furniture styling with velvet settees
Dietary Note: Fully dedicated plant-based and allergen-free stations available upon request.
All-Inclusive Luxury

The Grand Gala

Weekend banquets of 50 to 180 guests

$ 215 / guest

Multi-course fine dining, top-shelf bar with vintage Champagne, live jazz trio, and overnight estate suite stay.

  • 5-Course Chef Tasting Menu with premier Grand Cru pairings
  • Top-shelf open bar including vintage French Champagne
  • Grand floral arch and personalized wax-sealed menus
  • Live acoustic jazz trio or string quartet for 3 hours
  • Complimentary overnight stay in Bridal Estate Suite
Dietary Note: Custom 1-on-1 private tasting with Chef Julien to finalize all flavor profiles.
