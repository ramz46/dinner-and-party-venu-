Concierge & Location

We Look Forward to Welcoming You

The Rosewood Estate is located 90 minutes north of Manhattan along the picturesque Hudson River corridor. Private estate tours and sommelier tastings are offered Tuesday through Sunday by prior appointment.

Estate Address

Hudson Valley, Rhinebeck, NY 12572

Electronic Inquiries

Arrival & Valet Notes

Follow Route 9 north to Rhinebeck. Enter through the iron carriage gates at Mile Marker 14. Complimentary on-site valet will greet your vehicle directly at the front limestone portico.
